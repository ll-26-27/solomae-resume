# Solomae Getahun

A one-page, phone-first resume that an NFC card opens when tapped. Clay, oat milk and matcha colours, for ceramics, yoga and the café.

- `index.html` is the whole page: styles and the two small scripts are inline. Fonts are Fraunces, DM Sans and Caveat from Google Fonts.
- `resume.pdf` is the full one-page resume.
- `contact.vcf` is a contact card (name, school, email).
- `photo.png` is the photo.

No build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server`.

It started as the orange app in `ll-26-27/ll-week-11` (`nfc-app/app/orange`).
